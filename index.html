The House

One of Pakistan’s most recognised beauty houses.

Hifsa Khan Salon & Studio embarked on its journey in 2009 and has flourished into Pakistan’s premier beauty destination, revered for its luxurious bridal artistry and comprehensive beauty services.

Founded by Hifsa Saad Khan — a visionary entrepreneur who left behind a corporate career to redefine luxury beauty — the salon reflects her passion for beauty and excellence. We have dressed thousands of brides since 2009, and bridal artistry is still the heart of the house.
